FAQ

Is DROP! free to play?

Yes. DROP! is free to download and play. It shows ads between rounds, and a one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns them off.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Career, Training and the Daily Drop work without an internet connection. Only online leaderboards and friends need a connection.

When is DROP! coming out?

DROP! is coming soon to Google Play and the App Store. The store links will appear right here on this page.

Who makes DROP!?

DROP! is made by Benjamin Fauster, an independent game developer from Austria. Press and creators find everything in the press kit.
