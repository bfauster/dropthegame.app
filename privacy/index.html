Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · applies to the app “DROP!” (Android and iOS, app ID com.vsterstudio.drop), the web version of the game and the website dropthegame.app.

In short

  • Your game progress (career, statistics, achievements, crowns, look, settings) is stored on your device. Only if you choose Save Progress and enter your email address is a copy of your progress also stored on our server (cloud save).
  • For the Daily Drop online leaderboard, the game automatically creates an anonymous guest account. A random account ID, your player name (or a random name) with a four-digit number, and your Daily results are stored on a server in the EU. Name, score and rank are visible to other players.
  • You can add friends by sharing a friend code. Friends see your name and your Daily results. There is no search for players and no chat.
  • The Android and iOS apps show ads via Google AdMob: ads between game sections and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (e.g. for crowns). Personalised ads are only shown with your consent, which you can change at any time. After buying “Remove Ads”, no more ads are shown between game sections.
  • Daily reminders are optional local notifications that your device schedules itself. They are only active if you turn them on, and no data is sent for them.
  • With the setting HELP IMPROVE DROP! (on by default, can be turned off), the Android and iOS apps send error reports, stats about your first steps and data about your jumps (reaction time, parachute timing, result) as well as your character’s look and player level to our own server. The game uses no third-party analytics or tracking services and we do not sell data. This website sets no cookies and counts page views with the privacy-friendly service GoatCounter, which does not store IP addresses or track you across websites.

2. Data stored only on your device

The game stores the following data exclusively on your device (in the web version, in your browser’s storage):

  • career save, statistics, high scores, achievements, Daily Drop and training progress
  • crowns, unlocked and purchased cosmetics, your character’s look
  • the optional player name you choose
  • settings (sound, music, vibration, help improve DROP!) and tutorial status
  • whether Daily reminders are on and the hour of day at which you usually finish the Daily Drop (see section 7)
  • a counter for how often ads were shown and your “ad-free” status
  • the game settings last downloaded from the server (see section 3)
  • error reports, first-steps stats and jump data that have not been sent yet (see section 8)

We do not receive this data – except for the data described in sections 3 and 8, and a copy of your progress if you use the optional cloud save (section 3, “Saving your progress with an email address”). Settings, reminder data, the ad counter and your “ad-free” status always stay on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser’s site data removes it. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Section 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021, which does not require consent for strictly necessary storage).

3. Online features: guest account and Daily leaderboard

What happens?

So that you can compare yourself with others on the Daily Drop leaderboard, the game automatically signs in to our server with an anonymous guest account when it starts. You do not need to provide an email address or any other contact details. Without an internet connection the game remains fully playable; results are sent later.

In addition, the game downloads general game settings from the server once at start-up (e.g. ad intervals and the latest available version, to notify you about updates). No account is needed for this; only technical connection data is processed.

Which data?

  • Account data: a randomly generated account ID, access and refresh tokens (also stored on your device), time of creation and last sign-in.
  • Public name: the player name you chose in the game. If you have not chosen one, the game assigns a random name (e.g. “Swift Owl”). The server adds a random four-digit number (e.g. “#4821”) so that players with the same name can be told apart.
  • Friend code and friends: a random six-character friend code for your account, the accounts you are friends with and when you became friends, and how many times per day you tried to add a friend (to prevent guessing codes).
  • Daily results: date and version of the daily challenge, score and details of the attempt (points per jump, placement, number of perfect landings, best reaction time, false starts, crashes, daily twist, an attempt identifier), number of submissions and time.
  • Technical connection data: with every request the server processes your IP address, date and time and technical information about your device or browser (e.g. user agent). We use this only to establish the connection and to prevent abuse (e.g. mass account creation); it is not combined with your game profile.

Who sees what?

On the leaderboard, your public name with its four-digit number, score and rank are visible to all players. Your friends additionally see you in their friend list and on the friends leaderboard (name, number, Daily score and rank among friends). Your account ID, friend code, email address, cloud save, game details and technical data are not public. Your character’s look, player level and recorded runs (section 8) are not shown to other players yet. Please do not choose a player name that contains your full real name or other personal information.

Friends (optional)

Every account has a friend code. When someone enters your code in the game, you both become friends; sharing your code is up to you. There is no way to search for players by name and no chat. You can remove a friend at any time in the game (Daily Drop → Friends); the friendship is then deleted for both of you. Each player can have up to 50 friends. You can also get a new code; the old one then stops working and your friends stay.

To share your code, the game opens your device’s share menu (or copies the code) with a short text and a link of the form dropthegame.app/friend/?c=CODE. You decide where to send it; we receive nothing about this. When someone opens such a link on Android, the game opens with the code already filled in – the friend is only added after tapping ADD. The game reads your clipboard only when you tap PASTE. Sharing your Daily result works the same way via the share menu.

Saving your progress with an email address (optional)

Under Settings → Save Progress you can link your account to an email address, so that you can get your progress back on another device or after reinstalling. You do not need a password: the game asks our server to send a six-digit code to your email address, and you enter it in the game. Your account keeps its ID, name, leaderboard entries and friends.

  • Account data: your email address, whether and when it was confirmed, and the one-time codes (stored only in hashed form and valid for 10 minutes).
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress files (career, level and XP, crowns and unlocked cosmetics, your character’s look and player name, achievements, statistics, Daily Drop and training progress, tutorial status), a short summary (level and crowns) to compare it with the progress on another device, a version counter and the time of the last upload. The game uploads the cloud save when your progress changes and when the app goes into the background. Settings, reminder data, ad data and purchases are not part of the cloud save; “Remove Ads” is restored via Google Play or the App Store.
  • Sending the code: to send the email we use Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France) as a processor. Brevo receives your email address and the content of the email and keeps technical sending logs (e.g. delivery status) for a limited time. Brevo is based in the EU and processes data mainly in the EU; a data processing agreement is in place (Art. 28 GDPR). More information: Brevo Privacy Policy.

Logging out on a device (Settings → Account → Log out) only removes the sign-in from that device; your account, email address and cloud save stay on the server. If you log in on a device that already has its own progress, the game asks you which progress to keep; the other one is replaced. If that device was using a guest account, its Daily results and friends are moved into your account and the guest account is deleted. We use your email address only to send you sign-in codes, not for newsletters or advertising.

Purpose and legal basis

Providing the online leaderboard, friends, the optional email sign-in with cloud save and game settings as part of the game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); security, fraud and abuse prevention (e.g. checking unrealistic scores, rate limiting) based on our legitimate interest in fair and stable gameplay (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Service provider

We use the database and authentication service Supabase (Supabase Inc., 970 Toa Payoh North #07-04, Singapore 318992) as a processor. Data is stored in a data centre in the EU (Ireland, Amazon Web Services). Access from third countries (e.g. by Supabase support) cannot be ruled out; a data processing agreement and EU Standard Contractual Clauses are in place (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). More information: supabase.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

We keep the account, its email address, cloud save, friendships and leaderboard entries for as long as the account exists. Technical log data is kept by the provider only briefly for operation and security and then deleted automatically. You can delete your account including all leaderboard entries, friendships, your email address and your cloud save at any time in the game (Settings → Delete account, at the bottom of the page) or request deletion by email – see Delete account for how. We confirm deletion requests by email within one month.

If HELP IMPROVE DROP! is on, the first-steps stats only record whether the reminder offer was shown, whether you turned reminders on and whether you opened a reminder (section 8).

8. Error reports, first-steps stats and jump data

What happens?

To find bugs, to see where new players get stuck and to prepare a future online mode, the store versions of the app (Android and iOS) send the following to our own server (Supabase, see section 3) via your guest account, as long as Settings → Privacy & Data → HELP IMPROVE DROP! is on (default):

  • Error reports: the error message, the place in the game code where it occurred, a technical call stack, how often the same error occurred, the app version, the operating system name and version (e.g. “Android 14”), your device model (e.g. “SM-A546B”) and its graphics chip (e.g. “Mali-G68”). We use the device model and graphics chip only to find errors that occur on certain devices; they do not identify you. Before sending, the game removes folder paths and user names of your device from the texts. Each error is sent at most once per app version.
  • First-steps stats: once per installation and step, which of these steps you reached and how many seconds after the first start: first start, tutorial started / first tutorial jump / tutorial completed or skipped, career started, first jump, first league round, first promotion, first Daily Drop, first training jump, return on a later day and after a week, Daily Drop hint shown, daily gift collected on day 2 and day 7, reminder offer shown, reminders turned on, reminder opened – plus app version and operating system name.
  • Jump data: for each scored jump in the career and the Daily Drop: your reaction time after GO (or false start / no jump), how many milliseconds before or after the ideal moment you opened the parachute, landing time, damage, placement, game mode, league, jump height, health before the jump, the jump number within the round, a random round identifier and the app version; in newer app versions also the platform (Android or iOS), the random waiting time between SET and GO, your position in the league before the jump and your player level. The jumps are linked to your guest account; no device identifiers or location are included. The server combines the jumps of a round into a recorded run (five jumps, or fewer if you ran out of health).
  • Look and level: in newer app versions, your character’s look (the identifiers of the chosen colour, hairstyle, glasses, hat, parachute and other parts) and your player level are stored in your profile. The game sends them again only when they change.

We use this data to build a pool of real jump behaviour for a future online mode, in which you compete against recorded runs of other players, and to make the computer opponents play more like real players. In that online mode, your recorded runs may be shown to other players as opponents together with your public name and its four-digit number (as on the leaderboard, see section 3), your character’s look and your player level. Before the online mode starts, we will add a setting to turn this off and update this policy. Until then, none of this is shown to other players.

We do not collect any other usage data, do not track what you do in the game beyond what is listed here, do not create user profiles and do not combine this data with advertising data. Players who installed the game before this feature was added are not measured. The web version and test builds send nothing.

Purpose and legal basis

Fixing errors, improving the game for new players, balancing the computer opponents and preparing the online mode, based on our legitimate interest in a stable, fair and easy-to-learn game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning off Settings → Privacy & Data → HELP IMPROVE DROP!; the game then discards anything not yet sent and stops collecting.

Retention

The server automatically deletes individual jumps after 14 days, recorded runs after 180 days, error reports after 90 days and first-steps stats after 180 days. It keeps at most 100 recorded runs per account (at most 25 per game mode and league); older ones are deleted when new ones arrive. Your look and level stay in your profile as long as the account exists. If you turn off HELP IMPROVE DROP!, the game stops sending them; on request we delete what is already stored. All of it is deleted together with your guest account (see Delete account).

No automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Plausibility checks of submitted scores and jumps only serve to protect the leaderboard and the jump data against manipulation.
