Description

Short: DROP! is a one-tap skydiving race: jump on GO, fall as long as you dare, open your parachute late and land first.

Four jumpers wait on floating platforms high above the ground. READY … SET … and the moment GO! fires, you tap to jump – too early and you are out for a false start. Then the real test begins: the longer you free-fall, the faster you are, but open your parachute too late and you crash. Every jump is a split-second duel of nerve and timing against three rivals.

Win jumps to score points, finish rounds of five jumps on top to get promoted, and climb six leagues all the way up to the final showdown with the KING of the Sky.

Features

  • One tap to jump, one tap to open – easy to learn, hard to master.
  • Career mode: six leagues, promotion and relegation, Easy, Hard and Extreme difficulty.
  • Daily Drop: a new challenge every day with twists like fog, gusts or night drops, plus an online leaderboard.
  • Training: practice any height and analyze every jump to find the perfect opening.
  • Your jumper: hairstyles, glasses, hats and parachute skins – earn crowns and unlock exclusive items.
  • Progression: player levels, achievements and personal bests.
  • Play with friends via friend codes – and fully playable offline.

About the developer

vster Studio is the indie studio of Benjamin Fauster from St. Margarethen an der Raab in Styria, Austria. DROP! is designed, programmed and published by the studio and built with the open-source Godot engine.
